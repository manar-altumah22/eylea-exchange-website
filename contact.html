FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need an appointment? +

No appointment is needed. Simply walk in during our business hours and one of our staff will assist you immediately.

What ID do I need? +

A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's licence, or permanent resident card) is required for all currency exchange and transfer transactions.

Is there a minimum amount? +

There is no strict minimum for currency exchange. For international transfers, a minimum of CAD $100 applies. Contact us for details on large transactions as preferential rates are often available.

How long does a transfer take? +

Most international transfers arrive within 24 hours. Some destinations may take 2–3 business days. We'll give you a precise timeline at the time of your transaction.

Do you speak languages other than English? +

Yes! Our team speaks English, French, and Arabic fluently. We are happy to serve you in your preferred language.
